## Pre-history

**dBridge + Maldini** — formed **Future Forces Inc.**, became the **first act ever signed to Renegade Hardware** label. Debut: "Flash Gordon" (1996). Also released "Dead by Dawn" and "Tannhauser's Gate" (1997). Project ended 1998.

**Fresh + Vegas** — worked together as Fresh & Vegas, released "Otto's Way" on Metro Recordings. Dan Stein, Darren White and Maldini had also released tracks together as **Absolute Zero & Subphonics** on Renegade Hardware.

---

## Formation (1998)

The two pairs came together in 1998. Left Renegade Hardware and founded their own imprint **BC Recordings** (label name: "BC" on vinyl). First release: "The Nine / The Bridge" (December 1998, catalog BCR01).

---

## Key Releases

- **"The Nine / The Bridge"** (Dec 1998, BCR01) — debut single. "The Nine" voted #1 DnB track of all time by Knowledge Magazine readers (2008). The meaning of the title is known only to the four members.
- **"The Pulse / China Cup"** (1999)
- **"The Fear EP"** (1999) — inc. "4 Days," "Refuge," "The Fear," "Jellyfish" (w/ Matrix)
- **"Planet Dust / Speedball"** (2001) — released on **Prototype Recordings** (Grooverider's label)
- **"Inside the Machine"** (2000, 2xCD) — debut album. Described as "a defining statement of victory for robotic, post-apocalyptic Techstep" (Passion of the Weiss). CD2 was a DJ mix.
- **"Shot Down on Safari"** (2002, BCUK004) — second album. 2xCD / 4xLP.
- **"Book of the Bad Vol. 1–3"** (2001, BC Recordings) — EP compilation series
- **"Ice Station Zero"** (2018, RAM Records) — reunion album

---

## Sound: Techstep

Bad Company's aesthetic = **Techstep** (not Neurofunk, not Atmospheric):
- Robotic, mechanical, post-apocalyptic, relentless
- Evolving Reese-derived basses
- Razor-edited, functional, hard-hitting breaks
- Bleak, sci-fi atmospheres
- "What the hell is that?" screwface mentality (dBridge, RBMA)

Primary label affiliations: **BC Recordings** + **Prototype Recordings** (Grooverider) — NOT Metalheadz.

---

## 1998–2003 Timeline

1998: Formation + "The Nine"
1999: "The Pulse," "The Fear EP" / Fresh co-founds **Dogs on Acid** (UK's #1 DnB community website)
2000: "Inside the Machine" debut album
2001: "Planet Dust" on Prototype / "Book of the Bad" series
2002: "Shot Down on Safari"
c.2003: Group becomes inactive. dBridge and Fresh pursue solo careers.

---

## After Bad Company

**dBridge**: Founded Exit Records (2003). Solo album "The Gemini Principle" (2008). Co-founded **Autonomic** (2009, with Instra:mental) — 170 BPM hybrid blending DnB, dubstep, IDM. Mixed FabricLive.50 (2010).

**DJ Fresh**: Co-founded Dogs on Acid (1999). Founded Breakbeat Kaos (merged with Adam F's Kaos Recordings, 2003) — later launched Pendulum, Sigma. "Louder" ft. Sian Evans (2011) = **first-ever DnB track to reach UK #1**. "Hot Right Now" ft. Rita Ora (2012) = second UK #1.
