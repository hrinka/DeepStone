### Summary

This document is a call for proposals for a free online academic conference focused on bass culture and Afro-diasporic sound system traditions. It situates the conference within a growing scholarly field that takes seriously the cultural, political, and sonic dimensions of low-frequency music. The conference draws its name from A Tribe Called Quest's 1991 album and aims to move beyond romanticizing bass frequencies toward critical inquiry into their social and historical contexts. It highlights two landmark research projects — [[Bass Culture]] (2016–19) and [[Sonic Street Technologies]] (2021–25) — as evidence of growing institutional interest. The call invites 15–20 minute presentations on a wide range of topics, from [[Dub]] and [[Reggae]] history to [[Sonic Materialism]], [[Sound Systems and the Body]], bass and identity politics, and production techniques. The conference is organized through the University of Bristol and is entirely free and online. Proposals of up to 250 words were due February 10, 2023. The framing is notably interdisciplinary, welcoming music studies, humanities, and cultural theory perspectives.
