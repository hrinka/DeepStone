

### Summary

This blog post is a practical genre guide aimed at newcomers to rave culture, breaking down the major electronic dance music genres associated with raving. It opens with a listening framework — asking readers to attend to rhythm vs. melody, repetition, energy curves, and tempo feel — before walking through [[Techno]] (Detroit, late 1980s), [[Trance]] (Western Europe, early 1990s), [[House]] (Chicago, mid-1980s), [[Drum and Bass]] (UK, early 1990s), [[Psytrance]] (Goa/global underground), [[Hard Techno]] and [[Hardcore]], and [[Industrial / EBM]]. Each genre is described by origin, core traits, and typical performance spaces. The guide explicitly discourages gatekeeping and encourages listeners to follow feeling over labels. A recurring theme is that genres overlap and bleed into each other — described as tools, not borders. The post is commercially oriented (it's from a rave jewelry retailer), with each genre section linking to related accessories, but the genre information itself is substantive and accessible.
